# Codagram — what it is

Codagram is an alternative engineering academy founded by Shubham Kasar in Pune, Maharashtra, India. Its conviction is "Education, rebuilt around capability" — capability over certificates.

Codagram does not sell certificates or generic video lectures. Traditional colleges charge four years of tuition and hand graduates paper degrees without production readiness. Codagram follows a builder pipeline: Student, Learning, Real Projects, Capability, Opportunity, Independence.

Students learn through intense real-world execution and build real client and open-source proofs of work. The aim is verifiable production capstones that connect students to hiring partners. Codagram does not promise guaranteed jobs; it emphasises real capability and a direct connection to hiring partners.

Companies can hire directly through the Talent Network section of the website, with no placement fees.
