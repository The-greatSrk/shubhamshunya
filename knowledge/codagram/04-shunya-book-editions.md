# Shunya: Sat Chit Anand — editions, prices and how to buy

"Shunya: Sat Chit Anand" is written by Shubham Kasar. Its theme is the question "What remains when there is nothing left to prove?" It is a record of confronting ambition, borrowed identities, existential fear and the search for authentic freedom, rather than an academic lecture or textbook.

Editions: the Amazon Kindle e-book is available worldwide. The paperback costs Rs 499 (₹499) with free shipping across India. The Collector's Hardcover costs Rs 799 (₹799), with gold foil and an author inscription or signature.

Print editions can be bought on the website by scanning a UPI QR code. Supported UPI apps include Google Pay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM and Navi. After paying, the buyer enters the 12-digit UTR transaction number to receive a receipt.
