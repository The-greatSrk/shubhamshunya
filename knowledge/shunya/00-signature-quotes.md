# Shunya — signature lines from the book

> What remains when there is nothing left to prove?

> You are not searching for the self. The self is searching through you.

> Freedom begins when you stop trying to become someone.

> Build everything. Depend on nothing.

> Do your work with shiddat and samarpan, without making money the object of your existence.

> True strength is not when you withdraw from the world because you fear attachment. It is when you build the world with ferocious devotion, while remaining free inside.

> When you realize you have nothing to prove, your work does not stop. It transforms.

> Don't collect another certificate. Build something that proves you can do the work.

> A job can pay you. Capability can free you.
